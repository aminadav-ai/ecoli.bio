    About ecoli.bio

    Overview

    ecoli.bio is an interactive, high-performance web-based simulation platform dedicated to the metabolic modeling and analysis of Escherichia coli. Designed for both educational and research applications, the portal serves as a visual, intuitive laboratory for undergraduate and graduate systems biology courses, while offering research biologists and bioinformaticians a rapid, zero-installation prototyping environment for testing metabolic hypotheses in silico.

    Scientific Foundation

    The simulation engine is grounded in the genome-scale metabolic reconstruction iJO1366 (focused on E. coli K-12 central carbon metabolism). The platform implements Flux Balance Analysis (FBA) to calculate optimal steady-state metabolic fluxes, growth rates, and ATP yields under various conditions.

    To bridge steady-state optimization and dynamic biology, the portal incorporates kinetic constraints. Reaction boundaries are dynamically regulated using substrate concentrations and enzymatic parameters (such as Vmax and Km) via Michaelis-Menten formulations, allowing users to observe metabolic adaptation, feed-back loops, and accumulation bottlenecks in real time.

    Key Capabilities

    • Interactive Visualization: Explorable GPU-accelerated metabolic map rendering powered by PixiJS, displaying real-time flux magnitudes and direction.
    • Environmental & Genetic Perturbations: Modify substrate availability (e.g., glucose and oxygen supply) or tune enzyme parameters to simulate knockdowns, upregulation, or inhibition.
    • Guided Scenarios: Pre-configured interactive learning modules designed to demonstrate core metabolic principles, such as the anaerobic shift or pathway saturation.
    • Experiment Management: Secure cloud synchronization to save, load, and compare metabolic simulation profiles and history logs.
